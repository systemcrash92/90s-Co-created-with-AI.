## Assets in this repository

**All art and audio here is original or procedurally generated.** Specifically:

- **The 8x8 bitmap font** (`Seto90/Runtime/Text/FontData.cs`) is drawn from scratch for this
  engine, glyph by glyph, as diffable strings. It is not derived from any existing font.
- **The engine logo glyphs** (`Seto90/Runtime/Ui/EngineSplash.cs`) are original letterforms
  designed for this project.
- **All audio is synthesized at runtime.** There is not a single `.wav`, `.ogg` or `.mp3` file in
  this repository, and there never has been. Music and sound effects are generated from
  `SongDef` / `SfxDef` data by the engine's own synthesizers.
- **All visual effects are generated by code.** No PNG effects, no shader assets beyond the
  single inline CRT fragment shader.
- **The demo's sprites and tile atlas** (`DemoGame/`) were produced procedurally through MCP
  calls, as data.

No ripped assets — no fonts, sprites, tiles or audio extracted from commercial games — are used
anywhere in this engine or its demo.

## A note for people building games with this engine

If you import third-party art packs into your own project, record the author, the licence and
your modifications **before** moving anything into your project's `assets/` folder, and keep
that record in your repository. Renaming files on import erases their provenance; a pack whose
licence you cannot produce on demand is a pack you cannot ship. This applies to your game, which
is your content — the engine imposes nothing on it beyond the MIT terms above.
